Memos

A note-taking app for iPhone

Notes as tiles you can pick up and move. Write, sketch, record a voice memo, drop in a photo, a table or a block of code — all in one note, all on your own device.

Support

Found a bug, or something behaving oddly? Email mistydual@gmail.com and include the version shown at the bottom of the app's Settings screen, along with what you were doing at the time. It helps a great deal.

Privacy

Memos has no accounts and no analytics, and your notes never leave your iPhone. The privacy policy sets out the detail, including the one request the app makes over the network to turn a link into a bookmark.

Terms

Memos is covered by Apple's standard licence agreement for apps bought from the App Store.
