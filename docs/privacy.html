Privacy Policy

Memos for iPhone · Effective 20 September 2026

Memos keeps your notes on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, and nothing is sent to a server belonging to us — because there isn't one.

What Memos stores

Everything you put in a note stays in the app's own storage on your device: the text and its formatting, voice recordings, drawings, photos you add, code blocks, tables, and the bookmarks made from links. Your appearance and tile palette choices are stored the same way.

None of it is uploaded to us. We cannot read your notes, and we have no way to recover them.

Microphone

Memos asks for microphone access the first time you add a voice memo, and records only while you have a recording in progress. The audio file is written into the app's storage on your device and nowhere else. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone, which will stop new recordings from being made.

Photos

Adding a photo uses Apple's system photo picker. The picker runs outside the app, so Memos never has access to your photo library — it receives a copy of only the photo you chose, which it saves into its own storage.

Links and bookmarks

This is the one thing Memos does over the network. When you write a link on its own line, the app asks that website for its title and icon so it can draw a bookmark instead of a bare URL. The request goes directly from your iPhone to that website, using Apple's Link Presentation framework.

That website will see the request the way it sees any visit, including your IP address, and it is governed by its own privacy policy rather than this one. Nothing about the request passes through us. If you would rather not make it, don't put the link on a line of its own.

What Memos does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address is required or requested.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage measurement of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs — the app is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Backups

Memos does not sync your notes. If you have iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup switched on, your notes may be included in that backup along with the rest of your device's data. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and are not accessible to us.

Deleting your notes

Deleting a tile removes it along with any recordings, drawings, photos and bookmark images that belonged to it. Deleting the app removes everything at once. There is no copy held anywhere else, so there is nothing to request from us and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

Memos is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Substantive changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.
